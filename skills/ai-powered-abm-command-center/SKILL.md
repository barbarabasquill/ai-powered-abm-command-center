# AI-Powered ABM Command Center

Create an actionable ABM plan grounded in available account evidence. Optimize for buying-group engagement, Sales usability, and measurable account progression.

## Working Principles

- Distinguish verified facts, user-provided inputs, and strategic hypotheses.
- Never invent account news, intent activity, contacts, technology use, or opportunity details.
- Treat intent as directional evidence that must be combined with fit, engagement, relationship, and opportunity context.
- Use Bombora as an intent-data source within the 6sense workflow when applicable, not as a separate platform claim.
- Match personalization depth to the ABM motion: bespoke for 1:1; shared pain, segment, or use-case patterns for 1:few.
- Connect every marketing touch to an owner, Sales action, buyer role, and measurable next step.
- Exclude confidential or personally identifiable data unless the user confirms it is approved for the working environment.

## Workflow

1. Define the business objective, offer, target outcome, timing, and constraints.
2. Score accounts using fit, intent, engagement, relationship, and opportunity context. Explain the weighting.
3. Assign an ABM tier and state why the selected motion fits.
4. Map the buying group: business decision-maker, technical decision-maker, champion, evaluator, procurement, security, and potential blockers as relevant.
5. Develop an account or segment thesis based only on substantiated evidence.
6. Create a message architecture linking buyer pressures, value, proof, differentiation, and next-best action.
7. Orchestrate Marketing, BDR, AE, executive, partner, and customer-success plays across the journey.
8. Define content, channels, owners, triggers, service-level expectations, and follow-up timing.
9. Establish account-level leading indicators and pipeline/revenue outcomes.
10. Finish with risks, assumptions, open questions, and the next three actions.

## Output Standard

Deliver a concise executive summary followed by:

- Objective and success definition
- Account selection and tiering rationale
- Buying-group map
- Account/segment thesis
- Messaging framework
- Journey and coordinated play plan
- Content and channel plan
- Marketing-to-Sales handoff
- Measurement scorecard
- Assumptions, risks, and next actions

Use the repository templates when their structure fits. Adapt them rather than forcing empty sections.
